Technical Indicators Architecture

50 EMA (Exponential Moving Average)

Measures medium-term momentum. Reacts faster to price action than simple moving averages. When price sustains above the 50 EMA, the market exhibits strong bullish intermediate trend momentum.

200 EMA (Exponential Moving Average)

The primary institutional baseline for major market cycles. A Golden Cross occurs when the 50 EMA crosses above the 200 EMA, confirming a long-term bull market. A Death Cross occurs when 50 EMA crosses below 200 EMA.

1-Year Rolling VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price)

Calculates the volume-weighted average price across a rolling 365 calendar days (trailing 252 trading sessions for daily charts, 52 weeks for weekly). Institutions and liquidity providers use 1-Year Rolling VWAP as their true cost basis benchmark and equilibrium value zone.

RSI (Relative Strength Index - 14)

Welles Wilder's classic momentum oscillator evaluating the magnitude of recent price changes. Values above 70 indicate an overbought market with exhaustion risk; values below 30 indicate an oversold market ripe for mean reversion. The 50 level defines the bull/bear momentum midline.

ADX (Average Directional Index with +DI / -DI)

Measures directional trend strength irrespective of price direction. When the ADX line rises above 25, the asset is experiencing a strong, high-momentum trend. Directional bias is confirmed by the crossover of +DI (buyers leading) and -DI (sellers leading).

MFI (Money Flow Index - 14)

A volume-weighted momentum oscillator ("Volume RSI") measuring institutional capital inflow and outflow. Overbought readings above 80 and oversold readings below 20 indicate extreme institutional accumulation or distribution exhaustion.

MSI (Market Strength Indicator)

An advanced institutional composite oscillator evaluating price momentum normalized by True Range (ATR) and scaled by relative trading volume flow. A reading above 75 signals overwhelming bullish dominance, while readings below 25 reflect heavy institutional selling pressure.
